Focus that glows.

A Pomodoro timer with a neon dial: 25 minutes of focus, a short break, and a longer one after every four sessions. The whole interface shifts colour with each phase.

01What it does

Three phases, three colours

Electric blue for focus, mint for a short break, magenta for the long break — you can tell where you are from across the room.

Tuned to how you work

Set the focus and break lengths and how many sessions come before a long break. Breaks, and the next session, can start on their own.

History that adds up

Every finished session is recorded: today's count sits on the dial, and the history charts your focused time.

Keyboard first on desktop

Space to start or pause, R to reset, S to skip. Notifications, a chime and haptics tell you when a phase ends.

02No account, no tracking

Sessions and settings are stored on your device. Chronoflux has no account, no analytics and makes no network requests beyond loading the page.

03Get Chronoflux
